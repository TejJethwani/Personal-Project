01 Background

The MYP Personal Project

The Personal Project is completed in the final year of the IB Middle Years Programme. Each student chooses something they care about, sets a learning goal and a product goal, manages the work independently over several months, and reflects on what they learned.

This website is both the record of my project and part of its product.
