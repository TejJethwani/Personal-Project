01 Method

My research process

The same five steps for every topic, from a question to a change in my routine.

  1. Step 1

    Question

    Turn a goal into a specific question, e.g. "How much protein does an active teenager need?"

  2. Step 2

    Search

    Look for health organisations, universities and qualified professionals first.

  3. Step 3

    Evaluate

    Check each source against five reliability criteria before using it.

  4. Step 4

    Apply

    Change one thing in my routine and track whether it made a difference.

  5. Step 5

    Record

    Note the source, what I learned and how I used it in my process journal.

05 Secondary research

Research sources

Every source I used, what it told me, and how reliable I judged it to be.

How I checked reliability

Each source was scored out of 5, one point per criterion (the CRAAP test).

C

Currency

Is it recent, or still accepted? When was it last updated?

R

Relevance

Does it apply to teenagers and to my question?

A

Authority

Is the author qualified or the organisation trusted?

A

Accuracy

Is it backed by evidence and do other sources agree?

P

Purpose

Is it there to inform, or to sell me something?
