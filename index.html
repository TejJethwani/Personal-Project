    A three-month journey exploring exercise, nutrition, sleep, discipline and personal growth.

    My research process

    The same five steps for every topic, from a question to a change in my routine.

    1. 1

      Question

      Turn a goal into a specific question, e.g. "How much protein does an active teenager need?"

    2. 2

      Search

      Look at health organisations, universities and qualified professionals first.

    3. 3

      Evaluate

      Check each source against five reliability criteria before using it.

    4. 4

      Apply

      Change one thing in my routine and track whether it made a difference.

    5. 5

      Record

      Note the source, what I learned and how I used it in my process journal.

    What I noticed[Your observation]

    Exercise performance

    Poor sleep can reduce endurance, reaction time and the motivation to train.

    What I noticed[Your observation]

    Concentration

    Sleep helps the brain store what was learned during the day, which matters for school as much as sport.

    What I noticed[Your observation]

    Mood
