My Personal Project:Building a Healthier Version of Myself

A three-month journey exploring exercise, nutrition, sleep, discipline and personal growth.

For about thirteen weeks I followed a weekly training plan, rebuilt my eating and sleep habits around it, and recorded everything along the way: workouts, measurements, sleep logs, interviews and research notes. This portfolio presents that evidence in the order it happened.

Main goal

Follow a structured health and fitness routine for three months, document my progress with evidence, and create resources that other people can use to start their own health journey.
