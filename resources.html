02 If you're starting from zero

A 4-week starter plan

A gentler version of my first month. Build one habit at a time instead of changing everything at once.

  1. Week1

    Set up

    Record your baseline, choose 3 training days and pick a bedtime you can actually keep.

  2. Week2

    Build

    Add a fourth session if you're recovering well, and plan your meals for the week.

  3. Week3

    Track

    Log every session, your water and your sleep. Notice what gets in the way.

  4. Week4

    Review

    Repeat your Week 1 tests, compare and adjust the plan for the next month.
